01 / The roles

Different ownership. One outcome.

Both roles protect the member experience. They do it from different seats.

Member Strategist

Owns the relationship, the account strategy, and the revenue result.

Accountabilities
  • Understand the member’s business, priorities, and risks.
  • Set clear expectations with the member.
  • Own member communication and strategic escalations.
  • Turn feedback into clear decisions for the delivery team.
  • Protect retention and identify expansion opportunities.
  • Own the commercial outcome from request through paid placement.

Talent Advisor

Owns search execution, candidate quality, and the path to a successful hire.

Accountabilities
  • Translate the role into a practical search strategy.
  • Find, assess, and recommend qualified candidates.
  • Keep the pipeline accurate, active, and moving.
  • Prepare candidates for every member interaction.
  • Coordinate interviews, feedback, and candidate follow-up.
  • Flag search risk early and propose a better delivery plan.
The operating rule

The Strategist decides what the account needs. The Talent Advisor decides how to deliver the right talent.

03 / Decision rights

Who decides what.

When ownership is unclear, speed drops. Use this table before a small question becomes a member problem.

Decision Owner Required input What good looks like
Member priority and business outcome Member Strategist Member context and commercial reality The team knows why this hire matters now.
Search strategy and sourcing plan Talent Advisor Agreed scorecard and market evidence The plan targets the profile, not only the keywords.
Candidate recommendation Talent Advisor Assessment evidence and candidate intent Every recommendation has proof and honest risks.
Member messaging and expectations Member Strategist Delivery status from the TA The member hears one clear message and no surprises.
Scope, salary, or schedule change Member Strategist TA feasibility and market data The change improves fill probability without breaking trust.
Hard-search recalibration Shared TA owns the new plan. Strategist owns the member conversation. A dated decision replaces endless searching.
Candidate preparation Talent Advisor Role context and member expectations The candidate understands the role and can show relevant proof.
Commercial concession or escalation Member Strategist Revenue impact and delivery facts The decision protects the account and the business.

04 / The scoreboard

Measure the work that matters.

Activity is useful. Outcomes are better. Each role needs a small scoreboard tied to member and revenue results.

Member Strategist

  • Account healthRisk, confidence, and next action are current.
  • Member feedback speedFeedback reaches delivery while it is still useful.
  • Placed revenueOnly paid or placed hires count as closed.
  • Retention and expansionThe account stays healthy and grows for the right reasons.

Talent Advisor

  • Time to qualified shortlistSpeed without lowering the bar.
  • Qualified candidate ratePresented candidates meet the agreed scorecard.
  • Interview conversionShortlists turn into real member interest.
  • Candidate readinessEvery candidate is current, prepared, and intentional.

Shared result

  • Time to fillThe full process moves with purpose.
  • First Hire SuccessThe hire works after the offer is signed.
  • Rework rateFewer restarts, replacements, and avoidable misses.
  • Member confidenceThe member trusts the people and the process.

05 / Failure modes

When the model breaks.

Most delivery problems start as small ownership gaps. Fix them while they are still cheap.

No one owns the next action.

Fix it with one owner, one action, and one due date.

The promise to the member ignores the market.

The Strategist must use the TA’s feasibility evidence before making a commitment.

The search continues without learning.

The TA must bring a new plan. The Strategist must secure a member decision.

A candidate enters the interview unprepared.

The TA owns readiness. The Strategist supplies the member context that matters.

Feedback sits in a message thread.

The Strategist converts member feedback into a clear decision. The TA acts on it.

An invoice is treated as a placement.

Invoiced means invoiced. The process closes when the hire is paid or placed.
